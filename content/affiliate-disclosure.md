# Awl & Stitch Affiliate Disclosure

Awl & Stitch is reader-supported. If you buy through links on this page — or through links on any of our guides — we may earn an affiliate commission at no extra cost to you. This page explains exactly how that works, in plain English, consistent with the FTC's endorsement guides (16 CFR Part 255).

## How affiliate links work here

- Some links to AliExpress product listings are affiliate links. If you click one and make a qualifying purchase, AliExpress's affiliate program pays us a commission.
- **The price is the same for you either way.** The commission comes out of the platform's side of the transaction; nothing is added to your bill.
- Commission rates, cookie windows, and program terms are set by AliExpress's affiliate program, not by us. Where those terms matter to our coverage, we verify them from the program's own pages and date the check.
- **The links are marked.** Affiliate links carry `rel="sponsored"` (along with `nofollow` and `noopener`) and open in a new tab, so browsers and search engines can tell they're commercial links. No cloaking, no redirects that hide the destination.
- We only link to listings we have researched and documented with a date. We never link blind to a product we haven't checked.

## What this does not mean

- We are not owned by, employed by, or paid directly by AliExpress beyond the standard affiliate commission on qualifying purchases.
- Manufacturers and sellers **cannot pay us for a ranking, a "best" label, or positive coverage.** There is no paid placement on this site, and there never will be.
- A commission never overrides our criteria. If a listing dies, its seller reputation slips, or its price jumps, we remove or replace it — and log the change in the page's changelog.
- We don't get a better commission for pushing a worse tool. If a cheaper listing is the better beginner pick, that's what we recommend.

## How prices are verified and dated

Every price on this site is captured from the live listing during research and rendered as "price at time of writing" with the date it was verified. Money pages get a monthly price re-check, and the visible "Last updated" date and changelog line on each page reflect that work. Prices may change at any time — the AliExpress checkout page, including shipping and any import charges, is always the authoritative total.

## Where the disclosure appears

The standard box — *"Awl & Stitch is reader-supported. If you buy through links on this page, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We never accept payment for rankings."* — appears above the first affiliate link on every monetized page, not buried in the footer. Trust pages like this one carry no affiliate links at all.

## Why we think this makes the site better

The commission model lets us publish research-heavy guides without charging readers, locking content behind a paywall, or running display ads. It only works if you trust the picks, so the incentives point one way: keep the research honest, date everything, and say what we don't know. Our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/) documents how picks are made and how pages are maintained, and our [about page](/about/) explains who is behind the site.
