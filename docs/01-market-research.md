### 4.1 Why pricking irons win

- **Demand is multi-source and specific.** Buyers search “best pricking irons,” “3.38 vs 3.85,” “how many teeth,” and “WUTA vs Beagle.” These are purchase-adjacent questions, not idle hobby content.
- **The price ladder supports multiple articles.** A $23 MOZHE, a $27 WUTA, a $35 JOTUN, a $121 WUTA 20-tooth, and a $162 interchangeable-head set give real budget/mid/premium tiers.
- **The product is small and light.** That keeps parcel economics and returns saner than a stitching pony or a 74-piece kit.
- **Compliance risk is low.** It is a hand tool, not a medical device, battery product, or restricted category.
- **The SERP has one strong incumbent, not a wall of giants.** LeatherCraftHaven is good, but the AliExpress-specific and landed-cost angles are open.
- **Content space is rich.** Pitch, SPI, tooth count, obverse/reverse, steel type, handle shape, chisel vs iron, brand comparisons, wallet/belt use cases, and sharpening/care.

### 4.2 Why the backups are backups

**Stitching pony (3.95).** Good Amazon demand and a weak SERP, but the product is bulky, the AliExpress order base is thinner, and shipping/returns are harder to keep sane. It is a strong spoke or second hub, not the first money page.

**Beginner kit (3.90).** The strongest Amazon demand signal of the eight, but kits are heavy, quality variance is high, and the affiliate economics are diluted by low-price, high-piece-count sets. The content is also crowded with generic Amazon affiliates. It passes the threshold but is a weaker first bet than pricking irons.

**Edge beveler (3.75).** Good shipping profile and a clear use case, but demand is narrower and the SERP is more Amazon-retailer dominated. It is the reserve if the primary is rejected.

### 4.3 Why the rest are rejected as primary

- **Hole punch (3.55):** commodity, very low AOV, Amazon best-seller dominance, weak differentiation.
- **Skiving knife (3.40):** sharp-tool injury risk, low AOV, limited content depth.
- **Stamping tools (3.40):** custom-logo/IP risk, inconsistent kit quality, lower purchase urgency.
- **Awl (3.35):** low AOV, weak commercial intent, often an add-on rather than a destination purchase.

## 6. Current US import reality (2026)

### 6.1 De minimis is gone

CBP CSMS #66065494 states that, effective 12:01 a.m. on **August 29, 2025**, goods of all countries are no longer eligible for the administrative de minimis exemption under 19 U.S.C. § 1321(a)(2)(C), regardless of value, country of origin, mode of transportation, or method of entry. Formal or informal entry is required; informal entry is generally allowed for shipments valued at $2,500 or less.  
Sources: https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCBP/bulletins/3f01456 (accessed 2026-11-06); https://www.cbp.gov/trade/basic-import-export/e-commerce/faqs (last modified 2026-09-02, accessed 2026-11-06).

**Content implication:** A “$27 pricking iron” is not the landed cost. The article must explain that duties, fees, and carrier handling can change the total, and that the checkout total is the number the buyer should trust.

### 6.2 Tariff stack for HTS 8205

The 2026 HTS Revision 19, as modeled by Borderless CHB for entries on 2026-10-30, shows HTS heading 8205 (handtools) from China at an **all-in 37.5% to 55.3%** ad valorem range, including Section 301. The range varies by 10-digit line; some lines also include Section 232 metal derivatives. The Section 301 forced-labor action adds **12.5%** for China and is modeled as provision 9903.05.31; Section 301 List 3 is 9903.88.03 at 25%.

Sources: https://borderless.us/hts/8205 (page generated 2026-10-30, accessed 2026-11-06); https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-07-28/html/2026-15181.htm (Federal Register 2026-15181, effective 2026-07-24, accessed 2026-11-06).

**Important:** This is a planning range, not a classification ruling. Pricking irons could fall under more than one 10-digit line depending on material, edge geometry, and whether they are classified as edged handtools or another handtool. A licensed broker should confirm the exact line before the site publishes a precise duty percentage.

### 6.3 Shipping reality

AliExpress publishes no US-specific transit time. Its help center says the promised delivery time on the order is the commitment. Its disclaimed guide gives general ranges: standard economical shipping around **2–6 weeks**, faster premium shipping around **1–2 weeks**, seller processing **1–several days**, plus customs/local-carrier delay.  
Source: https://pango.ai/resources/how-long-does-aliexpress-take-to-ship-to-usa (2026-11-04, quoting AliExpress help center and guides, accessed 2026-11-06).

For this research, the AliExpress search cards confirm “ship from CN” but do not expose carrier, cost, or delivery estimate. That gap was closed on 2026-11-07 for the three shortlisted listings via the live US storefront — see section 7.3.

## 7. AliExpress listing evidence

### 7.1 Method and caveats

- Search pages were fetched with PowerShell `Invoke-WebRequest` using a desktop user agent. The item data is embedded in `window._dida_config_._init_data_` as `"itemList":{"content":[...]}`. A balanced-bracket parser extracted the JSON.
- `webfetch` returned empty on AliExpress search pages; item pages are JavaScript-only and do not expose seller, shipping, or variant data in the HTML.
- Prices are `prices.salePrice.minPrice` in **VND**, converted at **25,692.443974 VND/USD** from https://open.er-api.com/v6/latest/USD (updated 2026-11-06). Approx USD is rounded and should be treated as ±10%.
- The `pdp_npi` USD string is unreliable. Example: WUTA 74pcs kit `1005012068384452` shows a `pdp_npi` value of $440.73, while the VND sale price converts to about $72.67. The VND value is used.
- Search-card prices are **lowest variant** prices, not the price of the full set. A $1.20 pricking-iron card can be a single tooth, not a 2+8 set.
- Seller name, shipping method, shipping cost, delivery estimate, and full variant list are `unknown` for every listing. Free-shipping tags in the cards are inconsistent and are not treated as evidence.
- Order counts and ratings are AliExpress search-card displays, not audited sales.
