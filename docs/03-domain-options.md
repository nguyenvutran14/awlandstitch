# Domain & Brand Options — Leathercraft Hand Tools (G2)

**Phase:** G2 domain/brand selection
**Date:** 2026-11-07 (all checks below performed this date unless noted)
**Owner:** domain-strategist
**Status:** PASS — 20 candidates documented with dated evidence; 1 primary + 2 backups; temp `pages.dev` name chosen.
**Niche:** leathercraft hand tools for US hobbyists/beginners. Primary product: French-style pricking irons; backups: stitching pony, beginner kit. Brand must survive a pivot within this niche — no name is locked to "pricking iron."

---

## 1. Naming strategy

Three brand directions were generated, all constrained to: ≤15 characters, 1–3 words, no hyphens/numbers, US-English, easy to spell and say, no trademarked brand names (no WUTA, XOOL, Tandy, OWDEN, Weaver, Beagle, MOZHE, JOTUN or misspellings of any brand).

| Direction | Idea | Example candidates | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Coined compound (brandable)** | Invent a maker-flavored word from niche vocabulary: awl, stitch, smith, forge, guild | awlsmith, stitchsmith, stitchforge, stitchguild, awlbench | Most brandable and memorable; zero exact-match-keyword risk; expands to any hand tool, kit, or even leather care |
| **B. Two-noun conjunction (workshop feel)** | "X & Y" pairs built from tools + technique + workspace | awlandstitch, stitchandbench, benchandstitch, awlandiron, awlandbench, stitchandleather | Boutique, craft-workshop feel; both nouns are generic category words, so the brand covers pricking irons, ponies, kits, bevelers, awls — the whole niche |
| **C. Descriptive / technique-led** | Say what the site is: leather work, stitch bench, saddle-stitch lab | leatherworklab, leathercraftlab, thestitchbench, saddlestitchlab, stitchbenchlab, craftstitchlab | Clearest niche signal and best partial-keyword relevance, but higher trademark crowding and some imply a testing "lab" (conflicts with our research-based, no-fake-testing posture) |

**SEO stance:** all candidates are at most *partial* keyword matches (they contain generic words like "stitch" or "leather"). None is a spammy exact-match domain (e.g., `bestprickingirons.com` was deliberately not pursued). The recommendation is brand-first, per playbook.

## 5. Recommendation

### PRIMARY: `awlandstitch.com` — brand **"Awl & Stitch"**

- **Available:** RDAP HTTP 404 + DNS NXDOMAIN at Verisign/DNS, 2026-11-07 (appears available; unverified at registrar).
- **Trademark-clean:** no exact match on the USPTO federal register (mirror data refreshed 2026-11-06); closest mark (AWLAND) cancelled since 2018; no live similar mark in related classes; no business found using the name in web searches.
- **Niche-true and pivot-proof:** "awl" is the iconic leathercraft hand tool and "stitch" is the core technique — the brand covers pricking irons, stitching ponies, beginner kits, edge bevelers, awls, and later leather care or hardware, without naming any single product. It never says "pricking iron."
- **SEO-safe:** brandable phrase with partial keyword relevance ("stitch"); zero exact-match-spam risk; clean two-word brand for titles, schema, and social handles (X/YouTube handles likely free).

### BACKUP 1: `stitchandbench.com` — brand **"Stitch & Bench"**

- Available (same evidence, 2026-11-07); no exact TM match; no business found. Evokes the workbench — the mental home of every tool in the niche. Slightly longer (14 chars) and less distinctive than the primary.

### BACKUP 2: `thestitchbench.com` — brand **"The Stitch Bench"**

- Available (same evidence, 2026-11-07); no exact TM match ("THE STITCH" was cancelled in 2022). One caution: `stitchingbench.com` exists as a "Launching Soon" placeholder — a near-name neighbor, not a conflict, but worth knowing.

**Demoted with evidence:** `awlsmith.com` scored highest on brandability (8 chars, coined) and is available with a clean US register — but **awlsmith.co.uk is an active UK leathercraft maker** using the same name (common-law use since ≥2013, same niche). Using it for a US leathercraft site invites confusion and a potential rename. Rejected on risk, not availability.

## 7. How to attach the custom domain later (5 lines)

1. Buy `awlandstitch.com` (recheck RDAP first) — Cloudflare Registrar ≈ $10–11/yr at-cost, or Porkbun/Namecheap ≈ $10–15/yr.
2. Add the domain to the free Cloudflare account (auto-nameservers if bought at Cloudflare; otherwise change nameservers at the registrar).
3. In the Pages project: Custom domains → add `awlandstitch.com` and `www.awlandstitch.com`; Cloudflare issues SSL automatically.
4. Update site canonical/OG/sitemap URLs to the custom domain; keep the `pages.dev` URL as a redirect or secondary.
5. Submit the sitemap to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools under the final domain.
