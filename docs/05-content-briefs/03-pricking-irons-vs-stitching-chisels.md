## 5. Questions to answer (from real PAA/Reddit threads)

1. Are pricking irons and chisels interchangeable today? (Oct 2025 thread, top comment by u/Woodbridge_Leather: "terms are mostly interchangeable at this point… any you'll buy without specifically seeking those out will be suitable to punch all the way through")
2. Do pricking irons require an awl? (2021/2022 threads: traditional yes; modern French-style no — read the listing description and tooth length)
3. Which makes a smaller/stronger stitch? (Armitage PDF: pricking iron + awl gives the smallest hole and strongest stitch; punch-through irons remove beginner error; negligible on wallets)
4. Some chisel teeth are too short for thick leather — how do I know? (Jun 2022 thread, u/Uncomn1: "some stitching chisels teeth are too short to go all the way through thick leather"; check tooth length spec — Kevin Lee lists 8–10mm tooth lengths)
5. Why do my irons stick in the leather? (Aug 2026 thread: burrs/unpolished teeth; beeswax, polishing, metal-plate trick)
6. Can I get the French slanted look with a chisel? (2021/2022 threads: French-style "chisels" exist — again, labels are loose; look at tooth geometry)
