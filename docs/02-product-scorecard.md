## 1. Scoring method

Each candidate was scored 1–5 on the playbook rubric. Weighted score = sum of (score × weight). Minimum recommendation score is **3.8 / 5.0**.

| Criterion | Weight | What a 5 means |
|---|---:|---|
| US demand | 20% | Stable/rising multi-source demand, clear search intent, documented seasonal peak |
| Commercial intent | 15% | Buyers search “best,” “vs,” “review,” “for X”; high purchase intent |
| Affiliate economics | 15% | Competitive commission, long cookie, healthy AOV |
| Shipping & landed-cost risk | 10% | Light/small, low fragility, sane returns, acceptable duty impact |
| Competition | 15% | Winnable SERP: weak/dated affiliates, content gaps, no giant domination |
| Content/SEO potential | 15% | Rich entity/topic space, comparisons, FAQs, use cases |
| Risk & compliance | 10% | Low returns, low liability, no certification/IP traps |

**Important:** The “shipping & landed-cost risk” score rates the product’s physical/economic shipping profile, not whether a specific listing’s US carrier, cost, and ETA were verified. That verification is a separate hard-filter item and remains open.

## 5. Shortlist

### 5.1 Primary — French-style pricking irons (4.35)

**Why it wins:**
- Only candidate with strong demand across Reddit/YouTube, Amazon, AliExpress orders, and a dedicated buying-guide SERP.
- Clear price ladder: MOZHE ~$22.86, WUTA 1pc ~$26.95, JOTUN ~$35.17, WUTA 20-tooth ~$120.61, WUTA interchangeable ~$162.11.
- Small/light parcel; lower landed-cost and return risk than a pony or kit.
- Low compliance risk; generic hand tool.
- Rich content space: pitch, SPI, tooth count, steel, handle, obverse/reverse, chisel vs iron, brand comparisons, wallet/belt use cases.
- Winnable SERP: LeatherCraftHaven is strong, but no AliExpress-specific, landed-cost, or 3.38-vs-3.85 decision tool ranks.

**Who it is for:** US beginners and intermediate hobbyists making wallets, cardholders, belts, straps, and small bags; budget-conscious buyers who want French-style results without a $150+ premium set.

**Who should skip it:** Production leatherworkers who need hand-polished premium teeth and consistent long-run performance; buyers who refuse AliExpress shipping times.

**Content angle:** “Best Pricking Irons for Beginners: 3.38 mm vs 3.85 mm, Tooth Count, and Budget Picks” with a differentiated “$25 AliExpress iron + real US landed cost” angle.

### 5.2 Backup — Stitching pony (3.95)

**Why it wins:** Good Amazon demand (Lunarm 4.6/111/100+/BSR #109), weak/programmatic SERP, strong content space around jaw width, bench fit, and ergonomics. It is a natural spoke or second hub.

**Why it is not primary:** Bulky parcel, dimensional-weight shipping, higher return risk, thinner AliExpress order base. The Beech Wood listing has no public order count or rating; the 360° listing is a low-variant price.

**Who it is for:** Beginners/intermediates with bench space who stitch small goods and want both hands free. Not for long belts; a stitching horse is better.

### 5.3 Backup — Beginner kit (3.90)

**Why it wins:** Strongest Amazon demand signal of the eight (PLANTIONAL 4.6/2.2K/800+; Cridoz 4.5/2K/500+), clear gift intent, broad keyword space.

**Why it is not primary:** Heavy/bulky, quality variance, missing leather/mat in many kits, crowded generic-affiliate SERP, and lower margin after shipping. It passes the threshold but is a weaker first bet.

**Who it is for:** Absolute beginners who want one box and a guided start. Not for makers who already own tools or want quality over piece count.
